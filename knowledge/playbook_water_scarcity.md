# Citizen Playbook: Pipeline Burst and Water Contamination Action Plan

## Scenario Overview
A pressurized underground municipal potable water pipe has ruptured, flooding the street and draining treated drinking water, or tap water in residential units is suddenly arriving turbid, discolored, or smelling contaminated.

## Confirmed Municipal Channels
- PMC Care Grievance Portal: https://complaint.pmc.gov.in/home
- PMC Central Toll-Free Helpline: 1800 1030 222 (Operating hours: 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM)
- Official WhatsApp / SMS Complaint Desk: +91-9689900002
- Grievance Email: feedback@punecorporation.org
- Field inspection: the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office

## Suggested Follow-up Cadence (Citizen Practice, Not an Official SLA)
The schedule below represents practical citizen protocols; they are not statutory commitments:
- **Hour 0-1 (Emergency Water Alert)**: If water is gushing onto the road, call 1800 1030 222 immediately. State the exact landmark and request the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office, to close the sectional valve to stop water wastage.
- **Day 1 (Ticket Documentation)**: File a formal ticket on PMC Care under the relevant water supply category. If contamination is suspected, advise neighbors to avoid direct consumption until supply is verified.
- **Day 2 (Excavation & Sump Check)**: Verify complaint status on PMC Care. Check whether field technicians from the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office, have visited the site to locate the leak.
- **Day 3+ (Resolution & Flushing)**: After repair work is completed, request line flushing before resuming normal domestic water consumption.

## Citizen Tip for Swift Resolution
If reporting dirty or contaminated water, mention the specific color (muddy yellow, sediment) and smell, along with your consumer billing connection number and whether neighboring apartments are experiencing the same issue.
