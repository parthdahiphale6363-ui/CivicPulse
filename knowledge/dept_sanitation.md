# Solid Waste Management Department

## Scope and Mandate
The solid waste management functions of Pune Municipal Corporation operate under municipal solid waste management guidelines. They oversee primary doorstep collection, street sweeping, chronic open-dump clearance, and transfer station logistics across municipal wards.

## Sub-Issues and Complaint Taxonomy
- Missed primary doorstep waste collection by area collection vehicles.
- Overflowing community feeder bins and secondary transfer points.
- Illegal dumping of commercial, construction, or organic garbage on vacant plots or road berms.
- Stagnant street litter and lack of sweeping along public commercial markets.
- Open burning of dry foliage, municipal garbage, or plastic refuse.

- Photograph showing the volume of accumulation relative to adjacent landmarks.
- Specific location identifier (ward office, society gate name, landmark).
- Time of observation.

## Common Delay Causes and Escalation
Breakdowns of collection vehicles, transfer station maintenance, and access blockages by parked vehicles.
- Level 1 Escalation: the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office.
- Level 2 Escalation: Assistant Municipal Commissioner of the ward office.
- Level 3 Escalation: Municipal Commissioner / Central Grievance Redressal Officer.

## Standard 3-Step Resolution Procedure
1. **Route Triage and Crew Dispatch**: Complaint is reviewed and assigned to the local collection vehicle crew.
2. **Removal and Sanitization**: Accumulated waste is cleared, floor swept, and disinfectant powder applied to prevent foul odors: VERIFY
3. **Audit and Closure Verification**: Field completion proof is recorded and the ticket closed on PMC Care.
