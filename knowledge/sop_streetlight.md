# Standard Operating Procedure: Streetlight Fault Resolution

## Category Overview and Scope
This standard operating procedure governs the diagnosis, component replacement, and structural restoration of non-functioning LED streetlights, dark stretches, malfunctioning timers, and open electrical junction boxes managed by the PMC Electrical Department.

## Service Level Agreement (SLA)
- Exposed live electrical cables or open junction boxes: SLA timeline: VERIFY
- Individual non-functional LED luminaire / faulty driver: SLA timeline: VERIFY
- Entire street blackout or feeder circuit trip: SLA timeline: VERIFY
- Leaning or structurally broken lighting pole: SLA timeline: VERIFY

## Citizen Evidence Requirements
- Identification of the painted yellow/black alphanumeric pole number (if visible).
- Nighttime photo illustrating the unlit area relative to road features.
- Nearest landmark or crossroads intersection.

## Common Delay Factors
- Upstream distribution power failure from MSEDCL (Mahavitaran) feeder substation rather than municipal luminaire fault.
- Underground armored cable cut during third-party excavation.
- Dense tree foliage obstructing lighting reach requiring tree branch clearance.

## 3-Step Standard Operating Procedure
1. **Feeder Diagnostic & Circuit Continuity Check**: The electrical wireman examines the local feeder box, testing the MCB breaker and measuring voltage across phases: VERIFY
2. **Luminaire Replacement via Hydraulic Lift**: Using a vehicle-mounted hydraulic cherry picker, the electrician inspects the pole top, replaces blown LED drivers or fixtures, and seals cable glands: VERIFY
3. **Lux Level Audit & Ticket Closure**: The technician verifies evening illumination at street level, records photographic confirmation, and marks the task complete on PMC Care.
