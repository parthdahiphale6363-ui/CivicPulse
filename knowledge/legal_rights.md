# Citizen Charter & Municipal Rights

## The Citizen Charter Concept
A Citizen's Charter is an official public commitment made by an Urban Local Body (Municipal Corporation / Municipality) outlining the standards of service, timeframes for delivery, grievance redressal mechanisms, and citizen expectations.

## Fundamental Principles
1. **Right to Basic Municipal Amenities**: Citizens residing within municipal limits are entitled to access essential public services, including safe motorable roads, potable drinking water supply, sanitary solid waste clearance, functioning street illumination, and unblocked drainage systems.
2. **Right to Timely Redressal**: Every municipal grievance filed through official platforms must receive a unique tracking token and be addressed within the committed service timeframe specified in the local Citizen Charter.
3. **Right to Clean Environment & Public Health**: The maintenance of sanitary public streets, prevention of open solid waste decay, and containment of hazardous sewage are foundational duties of local administration to safeguard public well-being.
4. **Right to Transparency & Accountability**: Citizens have the right to know the designated officer responsible for resolving an issue, inspect progress records, and receive formal reasons if work is deferred due to technical or budgetary constraints.
5. **Right to Appeal & Escalation**: In cases of administrative apathy or substandard repair work, citizens are entitled to escalate complaints to higher zonal authorities and seek review through statutory grievance monitoring mechanisms.
