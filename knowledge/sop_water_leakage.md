# Standard Operating Procedure: Water Pipeline Leakage Resolution

## Category Overview and Scope
This standard operating procedure governs the identification, emergency isolation, and mechanical repair of burst distribution mains, cracked feeder pipelines, leaking air valves, and broken consumer ferrule joints managed by the PMC Water Supply Department.

## Service Level Agreement (SLA)
- Major trunk pipeline burst or violent street flooding: SLA timeline: VERIFY
- Secondary distribution pipeline leak on residential road: SLA timeline: VERIFY
- Minor ferrule joint or consumer connection leak at water meter: SLA timeline: VERIFY

## Citizen Evidence Requirements
- Video or clear photo showing the rate of water flow or street ponding.
- Exact landmark indicating whether the leak originates from under the road, footpath, or exposed valve chamber.
- Observation of scheduled water release hours when the leak intensifies.

## Common Delay Factors
- Underground water lines entangled with power or optical fiber cables requiring manual digging.
- Leaks located beneath heavy-traffic intersections requiring traffic coordination.

## 3-Step Standard Operating Procedure
1. **Supply Isolation & Traffic Protection**: The valve operator isolates the upstream sluice valve to stop high-pressure water loss: VERIFY
2. **Excavation & Pipe Restoration**: Repair crew excavates down to the pipe crown and fits mechanical repair split-collars or replaces pipe sections: VERIFY
3. **Line Re-pressurization & Leak Audit**: Sluice valve is reopened, line pressure checked for leaks, trench backfilled, and resolution logged on PMC Care.

## Escalation Matrix
- Level 1: Junior Engineer (Water Supply) at local Ward Office.
- Level 2: Executive Engineer (Water Supply), Swargate Pumping Station / PMC Main Office.
