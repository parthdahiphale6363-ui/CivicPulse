# Right to Information (RTI) Citizen Guide & Civic Application Templates

## 1. RTI Act Basics (All Public Authorities)
The Right to Information Act, 2005 establishes a statutory framework empowering Indian citizens to inspect public works, access certified records, and obtain official documents from municipal corporations and government authorities across India. VERIFY: fact not present on fetched source page.

- **Fundamental Right to Know**: Any Indian citizen can request factual records, sanctioned work orders, expenditure sheets, and tender documents. Speculative queries or subjective justifications are not covered.
- **Statutory Timelines**:
  - Standard response period: 30 days from the date of receipt of the application. VERIFY: fact not present on fetched source page.
  - Life and liberty urgency: 48 hours where information concerns life or liberty of a person. VERIFY: fact not present on fetched source page.
- **Section 6(3) Transfer**: If an application is submitted to an authority holding only partial records or an incorrect public authority, the receiving authority must transfer it within 5 days to the competent authority under Section 6(3) of the Act. - **BPL Fee Waiver**: Citizens Below Poverty Line (BPL) are exempt from application and copying fees upon producing a valid government-issued BPL card or certificate.
- **First Appeal Rights**: If information is denied, incomplete, or unanswered after 30 days, a First Appeal lies before the designated First Appellate Authority (FAA) of that department. VERIFY: fact not present on fetched source page.
- **Second Appeal**: If aggrieved by the FAA's decision, a Second Appeal lies before the State Information Commission (for state/municipal bodies) or Central Information Commission (for central bodies).

## 2. Central RTI Online Portal (Central Authorities Only)
- **Official Portal**: `https://rtionline.gov.in` (administered by the Department of Personnel and Training - DoPT, Government of India).
- **Scope**: Exclusively applies to Central Ministries, Departments, and Central Public Sector Undertakings. Do NOT submit state or PMC requests here (applications are returned without fee refund).
- **Character Limit**: Application text in the web form is strictly confined up to 3,000 characters. If the text exceeds 3,000 characters, it must be uploaded as a PDF attachment under "Supporting Document". VERIFY: fact not present on fetched source page.
- **Payment Modes**: State Bank of India internet banking, SBI ATM-cum-Debit cards, Master/Visa cards, and UPI.
- **First Appeal Rules**: No fee is charged for filing a First Appeal against an online application.
- **Record Retention**: Application status and history are maintained on the central portal for 3 years.

## 3. Maharashtra & Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) Process
- **Official State Portal**: `https://rtionline.maharashtra.gov.in/index.php` (an initiative of the General Administration Department, Mantralaya, Mumbai).
- **Covered Authorities**: The Maharashtra portal explicitly covers Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC), Pimpri Chinchwad Municipal Corporation (PCMC), Maharashtra Pollution Control Board (MPCB), and state administrative departments.
- **Filing Modes**:
  - **Online**: Registered or guest users can file RTI requests and First Appeals directly for Pune Municipal Corporation via `rtionline.maharashtra.gov.in`.
  - **Physical Filing**: Physical applications can be submitted at the Citizen Facilitation Center (CFC) at PMC Headquarters (Shivajinagar) or the concerned Ward Office (Kshetriya Karyalaya) addressed to the Public Information Officer (PIO).
- **State Application Fees & Modes**: Cash at CFC counter, Court Fee Stamps, Indian Postal Order, or online gateway: VERIFY.
- **Appellate Structure for PMC**:
  - **First Appellate Authority**: Designated Departmental Head / Additional Commissioner at PMC.
  - **Second Appellate Authority**: State Information Commission, Pune Bench.

## 4. Civic RTI Application Templates

### Template 1: Road Repair, Asphalt Quality, and Defect Liability Period (DLP)
**To**: The Public Information Officer (Roads / Public Works Department), Pune Municipal Corporation  
**Subject**: Application under the Right to Information Act, 2005 regarding road construction/repair work   VERIFY: fact not present on fetched source page.

**Information Sought**:  
With reference to the road resurfacing/asphalt work carried out on [Road Name / Stretch from Point A to Point B, Ward / Prabhag Name]:  
1. Please provide a certified copy of the Work Order and Sanction Letter issued for this road work.  
2. Please provide the name of the contractor/agency awarded the work and the total sanctioned expenditure.  
3. Please provide the Defect Liability Period (DLP) clause mentioned in the tender/work contract for this stretch.  
4. Please provide a copy of the Quality Control Inspection Report and Bitumen Testing Certificate submitted prior to releasing final contractor payment.  
5. If complaints regarding potholes or road degradation have been recorded during the DLP, please provide certified copies of notices issued to the contractor to rectify the defects.
