# Summer Water Scarcity and Municipal Tanker Operations

## Overview
During the hot summer months (March through June), rising ambient evaporation, declining storage levels across Khadakwasla, Panshet, Varasgaon, and Temghar dams, and surging domestic water demand frequently stress Pune's municipal water distribution grid. The Pune Municipal Corporation Water Supply Department implements seasonal demand management, rotational pressure adjustments, and emergency municipal tanker operations to sustain essential water access across peripheral and elevated suburbs.

## Water Scarcity Hotspots in Pune
- **Elevated Topographies**: High-altitude zones (such as parts of Kothrud, Bavdhan, Sus, Pashan, Katraj, and Kondhwa hills) where gravitational terminal head drops during peak consumption hours.
- **Peripheral / Newly Merged Villages**: Rapidly developing fringe sectors (Wagholi, Lohegaon, Manjri, Dhayari, Ambegaon) dependent on tail-end bulk feeders or localized borewells.
- **Groundwater Depletion**: Substantial drawdown of private and community borewell water tables across unconfined basalt aquifers.

## Municipal Water Tanker Logistics
1. **Government / Municipal Tankers**: Deployed free of direct civic cost to water-scarce municipal colonies, public hospitals, and slum pockets.
2. **Water Filling Filling Stations**: Water tankers draw treated water from designated municipal water filling points (such as Padmavati, Wadgaon, Parvati, and Yerwada tanker points).
3. **Private Tanker Regulations**: Strict monitoring of water source quality to prevent unauthorized commercial extraction of untreated agricultural borewell water for domestic supply.

## Water Conservation Advisories
- Prohibiting the use of treated drinking water for washing private vehicles, watering decorative lawns, or wet-cleaning building courtyards.
- Promoting rainwater harvesting maintenance and borewell groundwater recharge ahead of the oncoming monsoon.

## Grievance Redressal and Tanker Booking
- **PMC CARE Helpline**: 1800 1030 222 or WhatsApp 9689900002.
- **Requesting Tankers**: Residents experiencing acute water stoppage can request municipal tanker deployment through their respective ward water supply engineer or via PMC CARE.
- **Turnaround Timeline**: VERIFY: municipal SLA for emergency water tanker dispatch following confirmed piped supply failure.

## Suggested Triage Criteria
Suggested triage criteria, not an official standard:
- **P1 (Critical / Emergency)**: Complete water supply outage for more than 48 hours in a high-density settlement, hospital, or public health center.
- **P2 (High Priority)**: Chronic zero-pressure supply across an entire elevated residential cluster requiring emergency daily tanker deployment.
- **P3 (Medium Priority)**: Contaminated or muddy water delivered by an uninspected commercial water tanker.
- **P4 (Low Priority)**: General inquiries regarding annual dam reservoir storage percentage reports.
