# Citizen Playbook: Stray Animal & Dog Bite Risk Action Plan

## Scenario Overview
Aggressive stray dog packs, unsterilized stray animals, or cattle nuisance causing traffic obstruction or safety risks on residential streets within Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) limits.

## Confirmed Municipal Channels
- PMC Care Grievance Portal: https://complaint.pmc.gov.in/home
- PMC Central Toll-Free Helpline: 1800 1030 222 (Operating hours: 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM)
- Official WhatsApp / SMS Complaint Desk: +91-9689900002
- Grievance Email: feedback@punecorporation.org
- Field veterinary cell: the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office
- Medical emergency: the nearest municipal hospital or government health dispensary for post-exposure vaccination

## Suggested Follow-up Cadence (Citizen Practice, Not an Official SLA)
The day-by-day milestones below represent practical steps followed by active citizen groups to ensure accountability; they do not constitute official statutory guarantees:
- **Day 1 (Evidence & Filing)**: Note the exact location, animal count, identifying features, and whether any animals exhibit signs of injury. File a ticket on PMC Care under the relevant animal control/health category. Retain the generated numeric token reference.
- **Day 2 (Immediate Medical Action if Bite Occurred)**: If a bite occurred, immediately visit a municipal hospital or doctor for anti-rabies vaccine and medical evaluation. Report the incident directly through the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office.
- **Day 3 (Tracking & Reminder)**: Check complaint tracking via PMC Care. If unassigned, forward the token reference via WhatsApp/SMS to +91-9689900002 requesting dispatch of an animal control vehicle through the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office.
- **Day 5 (Ward Office Escalation)**: Call central helpline 1800 1030 222 to follow up regarding the Animal Birth Control (ABC) sterilization, vaccination, and tagging schedule.
- **Day 7+ (Formal Follow-up)**: By law under Central Animal Birth Control Rules, stray dogs captured for sterilization must be released back to the same territory once vaccinated and healed. If aggressive animal behavior persists, coordinate follow-up with the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office.

## Citizen Tip for Swift Resolution
Under the law, stray dogs cannot be relocated or harmed. Requesting "Animal Birth Control (ABC) sterilization and anti-rabies vaccination" in your complaint is processed legally by municipal authorities, whereas requesting "dog removal" is legally invalid and leads to ticket rejection.
