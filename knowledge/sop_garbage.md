# Standard Operating Procedure: Garbage and Waste Clearance

## Category Overview and Scope
This standard operating procedure governs the handling of missed doorstep waste collection, overflowing municipal garbage bins, scattered roadside litter, and accumulated organic refuse under the Pune Solid Waste Management mandate and the Solid Waste Management Rules 2016.

## Service Level Agreement (SLA)
- Overflowing street garbage containers or feeder points: SLA timeline: VERIFY
- Missed residential primary doorstep collection: SLA timeline: VERIFY
- Removal of public market organic accumulation: SLA timeline: VERIFY

## Citizen Evidence Requirements
- Clear photo showing the volume and spread of the waste accumulation.
- Identification of the nearest residential building name, prabhag ward number, or chowk.
- Information whether doorstep collection was missed by cooperative workers or municipal tippers.

## Common Delay Factors
- Route breakdown of waste collection compactor trucks or hydraulic tippers.
- Narrow lanes blocked by parked vehicles hindering compactor access.
- Non-segregated mixed municipal waste dumped during night hours.

## 3-Step Standard Operating Procedure
1. **Zonal Routing and Vehicle Dispatch**: The Sanitary Inspector verifies the GPS location and re-routes the collection vehicle to the reported site.
2. **Mechanical Loading and Surface Sanitation**: Sanitary personnel shovel accumulated garbage, sweep the perimeter clean, and spread disinfectant powder: VERIFY
3. **Telemetry Verification & Ticket Resolution**: The Sanitary Inspector uploads post-cleanup photo evidence and closes the ticket on the PMC Care portal.

## Escalation Matrix
- Level 1: Zonal Sanitary Inspector at the local Ward Office.
- Level 2: Divisional Sanitary Officer / Deputy Commissioner (SWM), PMC Central Building.
