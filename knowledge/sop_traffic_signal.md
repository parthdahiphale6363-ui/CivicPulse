# Standard Operating Procedure: Traffic Signal Fault Rectification

## Category Overview and Scope
This standard operating procedure governs the diagnosis, repair, hardware replacement, and timing synchronization of faulty vehicular signals, pedestrian crossing lamps, and junction controllers managed jointly by the PMC Traffic Planning Department and the Police.

## Service Level Agreement (SLA)
- Major junction signal blackout or stuck red/green lamps: SLA timeline: VERIFY
- Secondary crossroads signal blinker or timer display failure: SLA timeline: VERIFY
- Damaged signal pole or vehicular impact replacement: SLA timeline: VERIFY

## Citizen Evidence Requirements
- Name of chowk / junction and the specific road branch experiencing signal failure.
- Photo or brief video showing non-functioning LED aspect or blinking hazard.
- Approximate time of day the malfunction was noticed.

## Common Delay Factors
- Controller motherboard damage caused by voltage spikes during thunderstorms.
- Severed underground communication conduits during roadside pipeline works.
- Signal timing adjustments requiring coordination with Traffic Police.

## 3-Step Standard Operating Procedure
1. **Controller Inspection & Signal Isolation**: The electrical signal technician opens the road-side controller cabinet and tests incoming power: VERIFY
2. **Component Swapping & Cable Repair**: Burnt LED arrays, failed flasher units, or damaged power supply modules are replaced: VERIFY
3. **Phase Synchronization & Police Sign-off**: The junction cycle is put through automated test phases under observation of the on-duty Traffic warden and closed on PMC Care.
