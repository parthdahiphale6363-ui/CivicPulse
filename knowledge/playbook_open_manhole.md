# Citizen Playbook: Open or Missing Manhole Chamber Hazard Action Plan

## Scenario Overview
A drainage inspection chamber on an active road or pedestrian walkway is open, missing its cover, or severely cracked, creating an immediate safety hazard for pedestrians and vehicles.

## Confirmed Municipal Channels
- PMC Central Toll-Free Helpline: 1800 1030 222 (Operating hours: 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM)
- Official WhatsApp / SMS Complaint Desk: +91-9689900002
- PMC Care Grievance Portal: https://complaint.pmc.gov.in/home
- Grievance Email: feedback@punecorporation.org
- Field replacement: the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office

## Suggested Follow-up Cadence (Citizen Practice, Not an Official SLA)
The milestones below represent urgent citizen safety protocols; they do not represent statutory municipal promises:
- **Minute 0-15 (Immediate Physical Safeguard)**: Place an identifiable object or visible marker upright near the opening to alert passing vehicles and walkers.
- **Minute 15-30 (Emergency Telephonic Alert)**: Call PMC Central Helpline 1800 1030 222 immediately. State explicitly that an open chamber is located on an active thoroughfare. Provide the exact street name and nearest landmark.
- **Hour 1 (Digital Ticket with Photo)**: Send a photograph of the open void via WhatsApp to +91-9689900002. Log a ticket on the PMC Care portal under the relevant drainage category.
- **Hour 4-8 (On-Ground Check)**: Verify if maintenance personnel from the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office, have visited with a replacement cover or protective barricading.

## Citizen Tip for Swift Resolution
In your telephonic report, explicitly state whether the opening is on a vehicular lane or pedestrian path, and provide clear spatial landmarks to ensure rapid field crew location.
