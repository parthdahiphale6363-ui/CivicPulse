# Standard Operating Procedure: Pothole Resolution

## Category Overview and Scope
This standard operating procedure governs the inspection, emergency securing, and surface restoration of road potholes and asphalt surface craters on public streets within Pune Municipal Corporation limits.

## Service Level Agreement (SLA)
- High-Traffic Transit Arterial Road: SLA timeline: VERIFY
- Residential Colony / Feeder Lane: SLA timeline: VERIFY
- Monsoon Heavy Rain Periods: Temporary cold-mix patch timeline: VERIFY

## Citizen Evidence Requirements
- Photographs: one close-up displaying crater depth and one wide-angle frame showing surrounding street landmarks.
- Accurate GPS tag or nearest landmark / junction name.

## Common Delay Factors
- Active monsoon showers preventing bituminous emulsion adhesion and hot mix plant transport.
- Ongoing uncoordinated underground utility excavations without formal trench handover.

## 3-Step Standard Operating Procedure
1. **Site Inspection & Emergency Barricading**: The road supervisor conducts an on-site survey after complaint intake on PMC Care. Potholes creating vehicular hazards are marked with caution cones: VERIFY
2. **Debris Evacuation & Material Compaction**: Loose aggregate and puddle water are removed using blowers, tack coat applied, and asphalt compacted flush to pavement grade: VERIFY
3. **Quality Audit & Closure**: The site engineer verifies road level alignment, captures a photograph of the repaired surface, and updates ticket status on PMC Care.
