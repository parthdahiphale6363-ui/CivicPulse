# Citizen Playbook: Chronic Garbage Accumulation Action Plan

## Scenario Overview
A community feeder point, vacant plot boundary, or public sidewalk where solid waste is consistently accumulating, decomposing, and causing foul odor, stray animal feeding, and public health risks.

## Confirmed Municipal Channels
- PMC Care Grievance Portal: https://complaint.pmc.gov.in/home
- PMC Central Toll-Free Helpline: 1800 1030 222 (Operating hours: 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM)
- Official WhatsApp / SMS Complaint Desk: +91-9689900002
- Grievance Email: feedback@punecorporation.org
- Field oversight: the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office

## Suggested Follow-up Cadence (Citizen Practice, Not an Official SLA)
The milestones below represent citizen practice guidelines; they do not represent statutory municipal promises:
- **Day 1 (Documentation & Initial Filing)**: Take a photo showing the pile volume and its proximity to residential gates or commercial shops. Register a complaint on the PMC Care portal under the relevant sanitation/waste category. Record the complaint token.
- **Day 2 (Route Coordination)**: Send a WhatsApp message to +91-9689900002 containing the photo, token number, and building address. Inquire whether the local collection vehicle completed its scheduled morning route.
- **Day 3 (Sanitation Follow-up)**: If waste remains uncleared, call 1800 1030 222 to request a spot clearance dispatch through the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office.
- **Day 5+ (Chronic Spot Escalation)**: If waste returns daily, submit a written memorandum through the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office, requesting warning signage or increased collection frequency.

## Citizen Tip for Long-Term Resolution
Engage your Resident Welfare Association (RWA) or housing society committee to verify that all nearby residential and commercial units are handing segregated wet and dry waste directly to authorized collection workers, rather than leaving bags on street corners.
