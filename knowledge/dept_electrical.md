# Electrical and Street Illumination Department

## Scope and Mandate
The electrical and street illumination functions of Pune Municipal Corporation maintain public street lighting and illumination fixtures across Pune. The state distribution company MSEDCL (Mahavitaran) supplies power to the city distribution grid.

## Sub-Issues and Complaint Taxonomy
- Non-functional or flickering LED streetlights on residential lanes.
- Complete blackouts of entire street circuits or high-mast towers due to feeder trips.
- Day-burning streetlights remaining energized during full daylight hours due to timer malfunctions.
- Exposed electrical cables, open pole junction boxes, or leaning metal lighting poles.
- Electrical sparking on municipal overhead lines or low-hanging wiring hazards.

## Common Delay Causes and Escalation
MSEDCL upstream feeder power outages, underground cable severed during excavation, and tree branches blocking illumination.
- Level 1 Escalation: the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office.
- Level 2 Escalation: Assistant Municipal Commissioner of the ward office.
- Level 3 Escalation: Municipal Commissioner / Central Grievance Redressal Officer.
