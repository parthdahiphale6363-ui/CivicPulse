# Vector-Borne Disease Control (Dengue, Malaria, Chikungunya)

## Overview
Control and surveillance of vector-borne illnesses—predominantly Dengue, Chikungunya, and Malaria—are coordinated under the guidelines of the National Center for Vector Borne Diseases Control (NCVBDC) under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW), implemented at the municipal level by the Pune Municipal Corporation Health Department.

## Mosquito Vector Characteristics and Breeding Sites
1. **Aedes aegypti (Dengue / Chikungunya Vector)**:
   - Breeds primarily in clean, stagnant water collections (open overhead tanks, water coolers, discarded tyres, flower pots, uncovered buckets, and construction curing pits).
   - Day-biting mosquito with a short flight range, leading to localized cluster outbreaks in housing societies and neighborhoods.
2. **Anopheles (Malaria Vector)**:
   - Breeds in fresh rainwater pools, slow-moving clean water edges, and overhead storage tanks.
3. **Culex (Filariasis / Nuisance Vector)**:
   - Breeds profusely in polluted, stagnant drains, septic tanks, and sewage outfalls.

## Municipal Control Measures
- **Source Reduction**: Identifying and emptying stagnant water containers ("Dry Day" weekly protocols).
- **Chemical and Biological Larviciding**: Application of chemical larvicides (such as Temephos) or biological agents (Bacillus thuringiensis israelensis - BTI) in large water bodies, alongside introducing larvivorous fish (Gambusia) in urban fountains and wells.
- **Thermal Fogging**: Outdoor thermal fogging using pyrethrum extracts deployed during documented active cluster transmission or pre-epidemic containment.

## Suggested Triage Criteria
Suggested triage criteria, not an official standard:
- **P1 (Critical / Emergency)**: Multiple confirmed dengue or malaria hospitalizations clustered in a single housing society or school campus with identifiable active breeding sites.
- **P2 (High Priority)**: Massive open basement water accumulation or uncapped construction water storage with visible mosquito larvae breeding.
- **P3 (Medium Priority)**: Overgrown vegetation and stagnant water puddles along local road drains causing severe mosquito swarms.
- **P4 (Low Priority)**: Routine request for precautionary evening fogging in absence of reported cases.
