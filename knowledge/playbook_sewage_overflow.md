# Citizen Playbook: Sewage Overflow and Choked Drainage Action Plan

## Scenario Overview
An underground sewer chamber surcharging foul wastewater onto public pedestrian footpaths, vehicular roadways, or residential entryways, causing health hazards and potential contamination.

## Confirmed Municipal Channels
- PMC Care Grievance Portal: https://complaint.pmc.gov.in/home
- PMC Central Toll-Free Helpline: 1800 1030 222 (Operating hours: 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM)
- Official WhatsApp / SMS Complaint Desk: +91-9689900002
- Grievance Email: feedback@punecorporation.org
- Field dispatch: the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office

## Suggested Follow-up Cadence (Citizen Practice, Not an Official SLA)
The following steps reflect effective civic coordination practices; they are not official municipal deadlines:
- **Hour 0-2 (Safety First & Immediate Ticket)**: Photograph the chamber and contaminated flow. Note if water is entering basements or near drinking water sumps. Lodge emergency complaint on PMC Care under the relevant drainage category. Note your token.
- **Day 1 (Direct Telephonic Alert)**: Call 1800 1030 222 immediately. Inform the agent of the public health hazard and request dispatch through the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office.
- **Day 2 (Field Verification)**: Check complaint status on PMC Care. If unassigned, follow up via WhatsApp with token number to request crew dispatch from the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office.
- **Day 3+ (Post-Clearance Sanitation)**: After the blockage is cleared, inspect that the chamber cover is properly reset flush to the ground. Request sanitary squads via 1800 1030 222 to spray disinfectant over the affected area.

## Citizen Tip for Swift Resolution
Specify whether the choke is in the municipal public sewer line on the main street or an internal residential society sewer connection. Municipal authorities only service chambers located outside private compound walls on public roads.
