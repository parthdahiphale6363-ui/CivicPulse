# Standard Operating Procedure: Miscellaneous Civic Grievances

## Category Overview and Scope
This standard operating procedure governs the intake, classification, and inter-departmental dispatch of miscellaneous civic complaints that do not fit standard categories, including public park upkeep, stray animal nuisances, unauthorized hoardings, open public defecation, and missing street furniture.

## Service Level Agreement (SLA)
- Initial triage and department re-allocation on PMC Care: SLA timeline: VERIFY
- Removal of hazardous hanging hoardings or broken tree branches: SLA timeline: VERIFY
- Stray dog birth control / sterilization complaints (ABC Program): SLA timeline: VERIFY
- Public garden maintenance or broken park benches: SLA timeline: VERIFY

## Citizen Evidence Requirements
- Detailed description of the specific public hazard or civic discomfort.
- Minimum of one wide-angle photograph capturing the context.
- Landmark and nearest municipal establishment.

## Common Delay Factors
- Multi-agency overlap between municipal corporation limits, cantonment boards, and regional development authorities.
- Vague complaint descriptions lacking specific street addresses or photo evidence.

## 3-Step Standard Operating Procedure
1. **Intake Triage & Department Re-routing**: The Central Grievance Cell at PMC Main Office classifies the complaint and tags the appropriate technical department on PMC Care.
2. **Field Inspection & Action Execution**: The assigned ward officer inspects the site and deploys the appropriate crew: VERIFY
3. **Citizen Notification & Closure**: The responding department uploads field action proof and remarks, notifying the citizen via SMS and portal update.

## Escalation Matrix
- Level 1: Assistant Municipal Commissioner / Ward Officer of the respective Ward.
- Level 2: Deputy Municipal Commissioner (Zone Head).
