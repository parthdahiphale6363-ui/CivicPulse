# Citizen Playbook: Debris Dumping and Open Waste Burning Action Plan

## Scenario Overview
Commercial tippers dumping rubble, soil, and construction debris along open plots or roadsides, or unauthorized burning of leaves and plastic garbage in the open.

## Confirmed Municipal Channels
- PMC Care Grievance Portal: https://complaint.pmc.gov.in/home
- PMC Central Toll-Free Helpline: 1800 1030 222 (Operating hours: 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM)
- Official WhatsApp / SMS Complaint Desk: +91-9689900002
- Grievance Email: feedback@punecorporation.org
- Field enforcement: the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office

## Suggested Follow-up Cadence (Citizen Practice, Not an Official SLA)
The guidelines below represent citizen reporting practice; they do not represent statutory municipal promises:
- **Hour 0-2 (Active Smoke or Tipping Incident)**: If garbage is actively burning and producing toxic smoke near homes, call 1800 1030 222 immediately to request intervention from the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office. If vehicles are actively tipping debris, photograph the vehicle license plate from a safe distance.
- **Day 1 (Portal Ticket Registration)**: File a formal complaint on PMC Care under the relevant waste management category. Attach photos clearly showing dumped material and landmarks.
- **Day 3 (Enforcement Verification)**: Call 1800 1030 222 with your token number. Inquire whether the complaint was inspected by the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office.
- **Day 5+ (Mechanical Clearance Escalation)**: If debris blocks vehicular lanes or drainage channels, submit a formal written follow-up to the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office, requesting machinery mobilization.

## Citizen Tip for Swift Resolution
Never confront illegal dumping operators directly. Capture clear vehicle registration numbers and timestamps from a safe vantage point. Registration numbers allow enforcement officers to issue penal notices.
