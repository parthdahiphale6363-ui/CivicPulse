# General Administration and Ward Governance

## Scope and Mandate
The general administration functions coordinate citizen service facilitation, grievance intake, Right to Information (RTI) disposal, Right to Public Services (RTS) compliance, and administrative jurisdiction across the Ward Offices of Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC).

## Sub-Issues and Complaint Taxonomy
- Inter-departmental coordination delays and unresolved citizen tickets across departments.
- Unregistered public grievances or delayed processing of Right to Services (RTS) certificates.
- Encroachment by unauthorized hawkers, vendors, or illegal temporary sheds on public spaces.
- Unauthorized commercial display hoardings, flex banners, and defacement of public properties.
- Citizen facilitation center issues (birth/death registration, property tax payment counter delays).

## Citizen Evidence Requirements
- Name of specific Ward Office jurisdiction where the property or issue is situated.
- Photographic evidence of unauthorized hoarding or encroachment relative to road width.
- Existing PMC Care complaint token number if following up on delayed inter-departmental action.

## Common Delay Causes and Escalation
Multi-agency jurisdictional boundaries between municipal limits, cantonment boards, and regional development authorities.
- Level 1 Escalation: the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office.
- Level 2 Escalation: Assistant Municipal Commissioner of the ward office.
- Level 3 Escalation: Municipal Commissioner / Central Grievance Redressal Officer.

## Standard 3-Step Resolution Procedure
1. **Intake and Zonal Assignment**: Central grievance desk logs complaint and assigns token directly to the respective ward office on PMC Care.
2. **Field Enforcement or Service Delivery**: Field staff from the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office, conduct inspections: VERIFY
3. **Citizen Intimation & Audit**: Redressal note is uploaded to PMC Care portal and notification dispatched to complainant with inspection remarks.
