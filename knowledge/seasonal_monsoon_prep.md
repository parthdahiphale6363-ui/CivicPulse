# Monsoon Preparedness and Pre-Monsoon Municipal Action Calendar

## Overview
Every year ahead of the southwest monsoon (which typically arrives in Maharashtra in June), the Pune Municipal Corporation executes a comprehensive multi-departmental pre-monsoon preparedness drive. The drive encompasses nullah desilting, road excavation freeze, dangerous tree branch trimming, flood-gate inspection, and activating 24x7 disaster control cells across all 15 administrative ward offices.

## Core Pre-Monsoon Workstreams
1. **Stormwater Drainage and Nullah Desilting**:
   - Heavy earthmovers (JCBs, Poclain excavators) are deployed to remove silt, plastic, and debris from natural watercourses, river confluences, and culvert throats.
   - Silt clearance and chamber inspection along major arterial road drains.
   - Target Completion Deadline: VERIFY: official municipal cut-off date for pre-monsoon nullah desilting (traditionally May 31).
2. **Road Cutting and Trenching Embargo**:
   - Municipal freeze on granting fresh road-digging permissions to utility agencies (MSEDCL, MNGL, telecom operators) to prevent road sinkholes during rains.
   - Mandatory reinstatement of existing utility trenches with compacted bitumen/concrete.
   - Embargo Window: VERIFY: official dates for seasonal road cutting ban.
3. **Hazardous Tree Pruning**:
   - Trimming of weak, dry, or overgrown branches along bus routes and near high-tension electric cables in coordination with MSEDCL.
4. **Relief Shelters and Pumping Machinery**:
   - Identifying municipal schools and community halls in elevated zones as temporary flood relief shelters.
   - Pre-positioning dewatering diesel pump sets at flood-prone vehicular underpasses and low-lying riverside chowks.

## Citizen Grievance Reporting
- **PMC CARE Helpline**: 1800 1030 222 or WhatsApp 9689900002.
- **Reporting Incomplete Desilting**: Citizens can report uncleared nullahs, dumped silt left on roadsides, or unpaved utility trenches ahead of the rains.

## Suggested Triage Criteria
Suggested triage criteria, not an official standard:
- **P1 (Critical / Emergency)**: Unreinstated open utility trench across an entire arterial road carriageway heading into the monsoon season; choked culvert threatening immediate backflow into low-lying housing colonies.
- **P2 (High Priority)**: Accumulated heaps of excavated black silt dumped along nullah banks washing straight back into the channel during pre-monsoon showers.
- **P3 (Medium Priority)**: Overgrown tree branches rubbing against electric distribution lines outside residential windows.
- **P4 (Low Priority)**: General inquiries regarding ward-level monsoon control room contact numbers.
