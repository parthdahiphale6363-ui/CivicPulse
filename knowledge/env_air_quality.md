# Ambient Air Quality Monitoring and Smog Management

## Overview
Air quality monitoring in Pune is overseen by the Maharashtra Pollution Control Board (MPCB) and the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) under the provisions of the Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981. Monitoring stations across Pune evaluate major ambient air pollutants including Particulate Matter (PM10 and PM2.5), Sulfur Dioxide (SO2), Nitrogen Dioxide (NO2), Carbon Monoxide (CO), and Ozone (O3). ## Primary Emission Sources in Pune Urban Area
1. **Vehicular Emissions**: High vehicle density on arterial corridors (Karve Road, Hadapsar, Shivaji Nagar, Hinjawadi).
2. **Construction and Demolition Dust**: Uncovered transport of debris and ongoing excavation without wind-barrier netting.
3. **Open Waste Burning**: Illegal incineration of garden waste, plastic, and municipal solid waste at road edges or dumping grounds.
4. **Industrial Clusters**: Emissions from nearby MIDC industrial belts (Bhosari, Hadapsar, Pimpri).

## Suggested Triage Criteria
Suggested triage criteria, not an official standard:
- **P1 (Critical / Emergency)**: Dense industrial chemical fume release, burning chemical waste near schools or healthcare facilities, severe visibility reduction causing traffic hazards.
- **P2 (High Priority)**: Widespread commercial or municipal leaf/garbage burning emitting dense plumes over residential zones.
- **P3 (Medium Priority)**: Uncovered construction dust emission from commercial sites without water sprinkling or perimeter barricades.
- **P4 (Low Priority)**: General inquiries regarding continuous ambient air quality monitoring station data.
