# Standard Operating Procedure: Road Structural Damage and Reinstatement

## Category Overview and Scope
This standard operating procedure governs the repair of extensive road pavement degradation, including surface cracking, rutting, asphalt peeling, collapsed culverts, settled utility trenches, and broken pedestrian walkways under the PMC Road Department (Path Vibhag).

## Service Level Agreement (SLA)
- Deep sunken trenches or collapsed road crust: SLA timeline: VERIFY
- Bituminous re-carpeting / patch overlay on damaged road stretch: SLA timeline: VERIFY
- Footpath curbstones, paver blocks, and pedestrian ramps: SLA timeline: VERIFY
- Major culvert or bridge expansion joint overhaul: SLA timeline: VERIFY

## Citizen Evidence Requirements
- Wide-angle photo showing the span and depth of the damaged road section.
- Notable location markers (e.g., landmark restaurant, intersection, chowk).
- Note on whether the damage was caused by recent underground cable or drainage trenching.

## Common Delay Factors
- Coordination delays between trenching utility agencies (gas, electricity, telecom) and PMC regarding road cutting deposits.
- Heavy traffic congestion requiring nighttime-only asphalt laying permissions from Traffic Police.

## 3-Step Standard Operating Procedure
1. **Engineering Survey & Trench Stabilization**: Road engineers assess pavement base failure, mark the perimeter, and apply graded aggregate base course: VERIFY
2. **Sub-base Preparation & Resurfacing**: The damaged asphalt is milled down, sub-base compacted with rollers, tack coat applied, and asphalt overlay laid: VERIFY
3. **Pavement Level Verification & Sign-off**: Alignment and drainage slope are tested, edges sealed, and closure audit submitted on PMC Care.

## Escalation Matrix

- Level 2: Executive Engineer (Path Vibhag), PMC Main Office, Shivajinagar.
