# Traffic Planning and Transport Department

## Scope and Mandate
The traffic planning functions of Pune Municipal Corporation coordinate with the Police to plan and maintain road intersection infrastructure, pedestrian facilities, traffic signals, road barricades, and public parking spaces in Pune. While physical signal hardware belongs to the municipal body, traffic enforcement remains under police jurisdiction.

## Sub-Issues and Complaint Taxonomy
- Dysfunctional or non-cycling traffic signals at road intersections.
- Damaged, bent, or knocked-down signal poles and pedestrian crossing buttons.
- Broken road lane dividers, bollards, crash barriers, or spring posts.
- Missing, damaged, or unpainted speed breakers.
- Unauthorized vehicular encroachment blocking designated pedestrian footpaths.

## Common Delay Causes and Escalation
Power surges damaging local controller microprocessors; coordination requirements between police traffic management and municipal engineering crews.
- Level 1 Escalation: the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office.
- Level 2 Escalation: Assistant Municipal Commissioner of the ward office.
- Level 3 Escalation: Police Traffic Branch / Municipal Commissioner.
