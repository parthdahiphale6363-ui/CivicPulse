# Stray Animal Control, Rabies Prevention, and Carcass Disposal

## Overview
Management of stray animals and prevention of zoonotic diseases (predominantly Rabies) within Pune Municipal Corporation limits is governed under the Animal Birth Control (ABC) Rules formulated under the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act, 1960. Operations are executed through the PMC Veterinary and Health Departments in collaboration with Animal Welfare Board of India (AWBI) recognized non-governmental organizations.

## Animal Birth Control (ABC) & Anti-Rabies Vaccination (ARV)
1. **Humane Capture and Release**: Stray dogs are humanely captured using canvas nets, surgically sterilized, vaccinated against rabies, given an ear-notch identification mark, and mandatorily released back into the exact same locality from which they were caught.
2. **Relocation Prohibition**: Relocating stray dogs from their original territory to other areas is prohibited under Indian law as it destabilizes territorial packs and increases aggressive behaviors.
3. **Aggressive or Suspected Rabid Animals**: Dogs exhibiting clinical symptoms of rabies (excessive drooling, disorientation, unprovoked biting, paralysis) are quarantined by municipal veterinary squads for observation. VERIFY: mandatory rabies quarantine observation duration in days.

## Dog Bite Management and Medical Aid
- **First Aid**: Immediate washing of the bite wound with running water and soap for at least 15 minutes.
- **Post-Exposure Prophylaxis (PEP)**: Free anti-rabies vaccination (ARV) and Rabies Immunoglobulin (RIG) administration available at PMC hospitals, including Naidu Infectious Disease Hospital and ward maternity homes.
- **Reporting Dog Bites**: Reports lodged on PMC CARE to track aggressive packs for sterilization and vaccination drives.

## Stray Cattle and Dead Animal Carcass Removal
- **Stray Cattle**: Cattle causing traffic obstruction or safety hazards on public thoroughfares are impounded into PMC cattle pounds (Kanjipinjara).
- **Dead Animal Disposal**: Carcasses of large animals (cattle, horses) or smaller animals (dogs, cats) on public roads are removed by dedicated PMC Solid Waste Management hydraulic vehicles for scientific incineration or burial.

## Reporting Channels

- **Carcass Removal Turnaround**: VERIFY: municipal SLA for carcass lifting following complaint registration.

## Suggested Triage Criteria
Suggested triage criteria, not an official standard:
- **P1 (Critical / Emergency)**: Suspected rabid animal aggressively biting multiple pedestrians in a public street or school vicinity; dead animal carcass obstructing a high-speed arterial roadway.
- **P2 (High Priority)**: Stray cattle herd blocking a primary traffic junction or BRTS corridor.
- **P3 (Medium Priority)**: Unsterilized stray dog pack displaying territorial barking or chasing two-wheelers in a residential lane.
- **P4 (Low Priority)**: General inquiries regarding pet registration or renewal of municipal dog licenses.
