# Municipal Solid Waste Management Infrastructure and Processing Chain

## Overview
Solid waste management in Pune operates across an integrated municipal logistics network managed by the Pune Municipal Corporation Solid Waste Management Department in partnership with SWaCH (Solid Waste Collection and Handling) waste pickers cooperative. The chain spans doorstep collection, intermediate transfer, material recovery, bio-methanation, composting, and engineered disposal.

## Infrastructure Components
1. **Primary Collection**: Segregated doorstep collection of wet (organic) waste, dry (recyclable) waste, and sanitary/hazardous waste using motorized tippers and pushcarts.
2. **Feeder Points and Transfer Stations**: Intermediate aggregation nodes where localized collection vehicles transfer waste into large closed compactors.
3. **Material Recovery Facilities (MRFs)**: Sorting centers where dry waste fractions (plastics, metals, glass, paper) are categorized for recycling.
4. **Processing Facilities**: Decentralized bio-methanation plants producing biogas and electricity, alongside centralized composting and refuse-derived fuel (RDF) plants.
5. **Legacy Sites**: Scientific capping and biomining operations at the Uruli Devachi and Phursungi disposal sites.
6. **Capacity Metrics**: VERIFY: daily citywide municipal solid waste generation tonnages and plant processing capacities.

## Common Operational Gaps
- **Chronic Black Spots (Chowks)**: Open dumping of unsegregated waste at road corners due to missed collection schedules or commercial night dumping.
- **Compactor Leakage (Leachate)**: Malfunctioning leachate collection trays on refuse compactors spilling foul-smelling liquid onto public roads.
- **Secondary Segregation Breakdowns**: Co-mingling of previously segregated dry and wet waste into singular vehicle compartments.

## Suggested Triage Criteria
Suggested triage criteria, not an official standard:
- **P1 (Critical / Emergency)**: Smoldering or burning garbage dump emitting toxic smoke near schools, hospitals, or dense residential colonies.
- **P2 (High Priority)**: Massive overflow at a primary feeder point blocking pedestrian footpaths or spilling into open drains.
- **P3 (Medium Priority)**: Missed doorstep collection across an entire residential society or street for consecutive days.
- **P4 (Low Priority)**: General requests for additional community litter bins or awareness sessions.
