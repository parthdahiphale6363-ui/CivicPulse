# Environment and Tree Authority Department

## Scope and Mandate
The environment and tree maintenance functions of Pune Municipal Corporation handle urban ecological concerns, air quality, noise guidelines, and tree protection across Pune municipal limits.

## Sub-Issues and Complaint Taxonomy
- High-decibel noise pollution from commercial venues, construction machinery, or outdoor public loudspeakers.
- Dangerous, precarious, or storm-damaged tree branches overhanging live overhead power lines or roadways.
- Unauthorized felling or damaging of trees on public property.
- Construction dust emissions, uncovered debris transport, and burning of dry horticultural waste.
- Water contamination or effluent discharge into nullahs or water bodies.

- Decibel meter reading screenshot noting time and location for noise issues.
- Detailed photo of tree branch illustrating proximity to public roads or electric wires.
- Exact vendor name or construction project site address for dust/noise violations.

## Common Delay Causes and Escalation
Statutory requirements under tree preservation rules preventing cutting without prior inspection; police coordination required for noise equipment confiscation.
- Level 1 Escalation: the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office.
- Level 2 Escalation: Assistant Municipal Commissioner of the ward office.
- Level 3 Escalation: Regional Officer, Maharashtra Pollution Control Board (MPCB), Pune.
