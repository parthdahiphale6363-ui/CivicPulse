# Citizen Playbook: Monsoon Waterlogging and Choked Culvert Action Plan

## Scenario Overview
Intense rainfall creates localized road inundation, underpass flooding, or stormwater chamber backflow because roadside gratings or culverts are obstructed.

## Confirmed Municipal Channels
- PMC Care Grievance Portal: https://complaint.pmc.gov.in/home
- PMC Central Toll-Free Helpline: 1800 1030 222 (Operating hours: 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM)
- Official WhatsApp / SMS Complaint Desk: +91-9689900002
- Grievance Email: feedback@punecorporation.org
- Field action: the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office

## Suggested Follow-up Cadence (Citizen Practice, Not an Official SLA)
The guidelines below represent citizen reporting practice; they do not represent statutory municipal promises:
- **Hour 0-2 (Rain Inundation Alert)**: Take a photo or short video showing the water depth. If vehicles are stalling or water is entering structures, call 1800 1030 222 immediately and request de-watering assistance through the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office.
- **Day 1 (Grievance Registration)**: Submit a formal complaint on PMC Care under the relevant waterlogging or stormwater drainage category. Note the exact landmark of the clogged inlets.
- **Day 2 (Post-Rain Inspection)**: Once surface water recedes, check if roadside gratings remain packed with debris. Send a reminder with your token number to +91-9689900002 requesting desilting through the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office.
- **Day 4+ (Systemic Follow-up)**: If waterlogging recurs at the same chowk during every shower, submit a written follow-up to the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office, requesting a culvert and drainage review.

## Citizen Tip for Swift Resolution
Identify whether the blockage is caused by surface debris trapped on top of the grating or underground pipe choking. Clarifying this in your ticket helps field teams deploy appropriate equipment.
