# Urban Tree Protection, Trimming, and Preservation

## Overview
Urban green cover and tree preservation within Pune Municipal Corporation limits are governed under the Maharashtra (Urban Areas) Protection and Preservation of Trees Act, 1975. The statutory framework regulates the felling, transplantation, and systematic maintenance of trees across both public thoroughfares and private properties through the PMC Tree Authority.

## Statutory Guidelines on Tree Felling and Trimming
1. **Felling Restrictions**: Unauthorized felling, uprooting, or chemical burning/girdling of any tree in Pune urban limits without prior formal sanction from the PMC Tree Authority is illegal.
2. **Prior Permission Process**: Citizens, housing societies, or commercial establishments intending to trim overgrown branches or remove dangerously leaning dead trees must submit an application to the local PMC Ward Tree Officer.
3. **Emergency Pre-Monsoon Trimming**: Trimming of tree branches interfering with overhead high-tension electric cables or obstructing primary road traffic sightlines is coordinated between MSEDCL and the PMC Garden Department.

## Grievance Reporting Channels
- **PMC CARE Helpline**: 1800 1030 222 or WhatsApp 9689900002.
- **Reporting Danger**: For immediately hazardous branches or fallen trees during storms, citizens can report to the Ward Office or Disaster Management Control Room.

## Suggested Triage Criteria
Suggested triage criteria, not an official standard:
- **P1 (Critical / Emergency)**: Tree or heavy branch snapped and hanging over an active traffic lane, electric wires, or occupied dwelling during rain/windstorms.
- **P2 (High Priority)**: Ongoing unauthorized tree cutting or root cementing reported in real time.
- **P3 (Medium Priority)**: Overgrown dry branches rubbing against building balconies or blocking streetlights without immediate collapse risk.
- **P4 (Low Priority)**: Routine requests for roadside plantation or sapling distribution.
