# Standard Operating Procedure: Noise Pollution Regulation

## Category Overview and Scope
This standard operating procedure governs the monitoring, field inspection, and enforcement actions against unauthorized noise emissions under the Noise Pollution (Regulation and Control) Rules 2000, enforced jointly by the Police and the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office.

## Ambient Decibel Standards (Leq dB(A))
Noise limits under the Noise Pollution Rules 2000:
- Industrial Zone: 75 dB(A) Day / 70 dB(A) Night: VERIFY
- Commercial Zone: 65 dB(A) Day / 55 dB(A) Night: VERIFY
- Residential Zone: 55 dB(A) Day / 45 dB(A) Night: VERIFY
- Silence Zone (100 meters around hospitals, educational institutions, courts): 50 dB(A) Day / 40 dB(A) Night: VERIFY
- Day time (6:00 AM to 10:00 PM) and Night time (10:00 PM to 6:00 AM) definitions: VERIFY

- Decibel meter reading screenshot noting peak Leq dB level.

- Date, duration, and time of persistent violation.

## Common Delay Factors
- Mobile sound systems relocating prior to police arrival.
- Special festival exemptions issued by the District Collectorate for designated calendar days.

## 3-Step Standard Operating Procedure
1. **Immediate Police Dispatch & Source Identification**: The local police mobile patrol visits the GPS coordinates to inspect whether the source has valid permission: VERIFY
2. **Decibel Measurement & Equipment Seizure**: If sound levels exceed prescribed decibel limits, the police officer issues an order to cease operation: VERIFY
3. **Challan Issuance & Zonal Registration**: A formal panchnama or compounded fine challan is issued and recorded in the police registry: VERIFY
