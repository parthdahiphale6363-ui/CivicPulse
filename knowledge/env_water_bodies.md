# Water Bodies Preservation and Pollution Control (Rivers, Lakes, Nullahs)

## Overview
Pune's urban water ecosystem is centered around the Mula and Mutha rivers, their confluence at Sangam Bridge (forming the Mula-Mutha river), tributary streams, urban lakes (such as Katraj and Pashan lakes), and an extensive natural stormwater nullah drainage network. Preservation and pollution abatement of these water resources fall under the Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974, administered by the Maharashtra Pollution Control Board (MPCB) alongside the Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) Environment Department. ## Common Water Body Violations
1. **Direct Untreated Sewage Inflow**: Outfalls discharging domestic blackwater and greywater directly into natural streams and riverbeds without prior municipal sewage treatment plant processing.
2. **Industrial Effluent Discharge**: Illegal dumping of chemical wastewater or tank washing residues into stormwater nullahs.
3. **Debris and Solid Waste Dumping**: Construction and demolition (C&D) waste, plastic garbage, and religious offerings discarded along riverbanks and nullah beds, obstructing natural floodplains.
4. **Floodplain Encroachment**: Construction within the river blue line (prohibitive zone) or red line (restrictive zone).

## Suggested Triage Criteria
Suggested triage criteria, not an official standard:
- **P1 (Critical / Emergency)**: Direct chemical or industrial discharge causing visible discolouration, pungent toxic fumes, or mass fish mortality.
- **P2 (High Priority)**: Major raw municipal sewage pipeline rupture discharging directly into drinking water source catchments or lakes.
- **P3 (Medium Priority)**: Persistent solid waste and plastic accumulation choking a natural stormwater nullah ahead of the monsoon season.
- **P4 (Low Priority)**: General inquiries regarding lake restoration or beautification projects.
