# Roads, Pavements, Bridges, and Flyover Infrastructure

## Overview
Pune Municipal Corporation's road network consists of major arterial corridors, radial bypass roads, ring routes, internal ward streets, and flyover structures. The structural integrity of the pavement network is maintained under IRC (Indian Roads Congress) standards, managed through PMC's Road and Traffic Planning departments.

## Pavement Anatomy and Construction Layers
1. **Subgrade**: Compacted natural soil bed providing the foundational support.
2. **Sub-base and Base**: Granular Sub-Base (GSB) and Wet Mix Macadam (WMM) distributing vehicular loads.
3. **Bituminous / Concrete Surface**: Dense Bituminous Macadam (DBM) topped with Bituminous Concrete (BC) or Mastic Asphalt for flexible pavements, or concrete slabs for rigid pavements.
4. **Engineering Standards**: VERIFY: design axle load norms and mandatory pavement curing periods under IRC specifications.

## Pothole Formation and Repair Methodologies
- **Water Ingress**: Moisture penetrating micro-cracks in asphalt weakens the adhesion between aggregate and bitumen. Repeated heavy vehicular traffic under hydraulic pressure blows out surface material, creating potholes.
- **Cold-Mix Asphalt**: Pre-mixed asphalt compound used for rapid emergency filling during active monsoon rains when hot bitumen plants cannot operate.
- **Hot-Mix Patching**: Permanent dry-weather repair requiring edge cutting, cleaning, tack coating, hot bituminous aggregate filling, and roller compaction.
- **Utility Trench Settlement**: Uncompacted soil backfill following water, power, or optic fiber cable trenching causing linear road collapse.

## Suggested Triage Criteria
Suggested triage criteria, not an official standard:
- **P1 (Critical / Emergency)**: Deep crater or road collapse (cave-in) on an arterial corridor or bridge deck causing immediate risk of two-wheeler crashes.
- **P2 (High Priority)**: Severe cluster of potholes at a busy signalized junction slowing traffic and generating gridlock.
- **P3 (Medium Priority)**: Uneven utility trench settlement or raised chamber edge causing vehicle jolting on local residential streets.
- **P4 (Low Priority)**: Faded thermoplastic road markings, lane dividers, or curb paint touch-ups.
