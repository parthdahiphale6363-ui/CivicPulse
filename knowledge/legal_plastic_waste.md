# Plastic Waste Management Rules: Prohibited Items & Citizen Compliance

## Regulatory Framework
Under the Plastic Waste Management Amendment Rules, 2021 notified by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, Government of India, identified single-use plastic items having low utility and high littering potential are prohibited.

## Prohibited Single-Use Plastic Items
The manufacture, import, stocking, distribution, sale and use of the following single-use plastic commodities (including polystyrene and expanded polystyrene) are prohibited with effect from 1st July, 2022:
1. **Earbuds & Sticks**: Ear buds with plastic sticks, plastic sticks for balloons, plastic flags, candy sticks, ice-cream sticks, and polystyrene [Thermocol] for decoration.
2. **Cutlery & Tableware**: Plates, cups, glasses, cutlery such as forks, spoons, knives, straw, trays, and stirrers.
3. **Packaging & Wrapping**: Wrapping or packing films around sweet boxes, invitation cards, and cigarette packets.
4. **Banners**: Plastic or PVC banners less than 100 micron.

## Carry Bag Thickness Regulations
To curb littering from lightweight plastic carry bags:
- The thickness of plastic carry bags was increased from 50 microns to 75 microns with effect from 30th September, 2021.
- The thickness was further increased to 120 microns with effect from 31st December, 2022.

## Citizen Rights and Reporting
- Citizens witnessing illegal manufacture, stocking, or sale of banned single-use plastic items can report violations through municipal portals or the state pollution control board.
