# Standard Operating Procedure: Sewer Overflow and Drainage Resolution

## Category Overview and Scope
This standard operating procedure governs the clearance of choked underground sewer lines, backflowing manholes, contaminated surface run-off, and damaged sewage inspection chambers overseen by the PMC Drainage Maintenance Department.

## Service Level Agreement (SLA)
- Open or missing sewer manhole cover: SLA timeline: VERIFY
- Overflowing sewer surcharging onto active public roads or residences: SLA timeline: VERIFY
- Internal lane sewer blockages / slow draining: SLA timeline: VERIFY
- Broken chamber masonry reconstruction: SLA timeline: VERIFY

## Citizen Evidence Requirements
- Photo or video of the overflowing manhole showing street contamination and nearby properties.
- Exact landmark (house number, shop board, colony lane).
- Indication if the manhole cover is missing, cracked, or dislodged.

## Common Delay Factors
- Hard solid waste, plastic bags, or commercial grease solidified in the sewer line.
- Stormwater ingress flooding the sewer network during monsoon downpours.
- Strict requirement for mechanized jetting under manual scavenging prohibition laws.

## 3-Step Standard Operating Procedure
1. **Safety Barricading & Mechanized Dispatch**: The Drainage Supervisor places caution barricades over missing or flooded chambers, and requests a jetting-cum-suction machine: VERIFY
2. **High-Pressure Hydro-Jetting & Vacuum De-silting**: The jetting nozzle breaks grease cakes while the vacuum hose extracts accumulated sludge into the tanker: VERIFY
3. **Flow Clearance Verification & Disinfection**: Gravity flow is inspected between adjacent manholes, cover seated flush, surface sprayed with disinfectant, and resolution logged on PMC Care.
