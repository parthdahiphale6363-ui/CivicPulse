# Citizen Playbook: Footpath Encroachment & Illegal Flex Action Plan

## Scenario Overview
Illegal obstruction of public footpaths, unauthorized structures blocking pedestrian right-of-way, or hazardous flex hoardings erected on traffic junctions within Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) limits.

## Confirmed Municipal Channels
- PMC Care Grievance Portal: https://complaint.pmc.gov.in/home
- PMC Central Toll-Free Helpline: 1800 1030 222 (Operating hours: 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM)
- Official WhatsApp / SMS Complaint Desk: +91-9689900002
- Grievance Email: feedback@punecorporation.org
- Field enforcement: the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office
- Immediate Traffic Hazard / Obstruction: Maharashtra Police Helpline 112

## Suggested Follow-up Cadence (Citizen Practice, Not an Official SLA)
The day-by-day milestones below represent practical steps followed by active citizen groups to ensure accountability; they do not constitute official statutory guarantees:
- **Day 1 (Evidence & Filing)**: Photograph the obstruction clearly showing pedestrian blockage or structural hazard. Lodge grievance on PMC Care under the relevant encroachment/signage category. Note the tracking token.
- **Day 3 (First Status Check)**: Check status on PMC Care portal. If marked unassigned, message the token number to +91-9689900002 emphasizing pedestrian safety risk.
- **Day 5 (Ward Office Engagement)**: Call 1800 1030 222 to follow up on the inspection schedule through the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office.
- **Day 7+ (Escalation)**: Submit a formal written memorandum through the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office. For heavy flex hoardings threatening electric wires or public safety, file a parallel safety complaint with MSEDCL (1800-233-3435) or Police (112).

## Citizen Tip for Swift Resolution
Focus on public pedestrian safety in your complaint text. When reporting illegal banners or flex boards, note whether they lack mandatory municipal permission stamps.
