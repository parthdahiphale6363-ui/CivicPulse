# Citizen Playbook: Noise Violation & Loudspeaker Disturbance Action Plan

## Scenario Overview
Excessive nighttime noise pollution exceeding statutory limits under the Noise Pollution (Regulation and Control) Rules, 2000, caused by unauthorized loudspeakers, late-night DJ music, construction activity during prohibited hours, or heavy commercial generators near residential areas within Pune.

## Ambient Decibel Standards (Noise Pollution Rules, 2000)
- Residential Area: Day limit 55 dB(A) Leq / Night limit 45 dB(A) Leq: VERIFY
- Commercial Area: Day limit 65 dB(A) Leq / Night limit 55 dB(A) Leq: VERIFY
- Silence Zone (100 meters around hospitals, educational institutions, courts): Day limit 50 dB(A) / Night limit 40 dB(A): VERIFY
- Nighttime restrictions (10:00 PM to 6:00 AM) on public address systems: VERIFY

## Confirmed Enforcement Channels
- Immediate Nighttime Police Intervention: Maharashtra Police Emergency Helpline 112 / 100
- PMC Care Grievance Portal: https://complaint.pmc.gov.in/home
- PMC Central Toll-Free Helpline: 1800 1030 222 (Operating hours: 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM)
- Official WhatsApp / SMS Complaint Desk: +91-9689900002
- Grievance Email: feedback@punecorporation.org
- Higher escalation for chronic industrial noise: Maharashtra Pollution Control Board (MPCB)

## Suggested Follow-up Cadence (Citizen Practice, Not an Official SLA)
The day-by-day milestones below represent practical steps followed by active citizen groups to ensure accountability; they do not constitute official statutory guarantees:
- **Night 1 (Real-Time Police Report)**: When violations occur after 10:00 PM, dial 112 immediately. Note the exact incident time, street address, and source (e.g. banquet hall, construction site, open ground). Request police dispatch to stop the sound system. Note the 112 event ID.
- **Day 2 (Civic Ticket Filing)**: If noise stems from commercial rooftop pubs, construction machinery, or diesel generators, lodge a formal ticket on PMC Care attaching video/audio evidence and quoting the 112 call reference.
- **Day 4 (Ward & Police Chowky Follow-up)**: If violation is recurring, visit the local police chowky with fellow society residents. Submit a written complaint letter addressed to the Senior Police Inspector citing the Noise Pollution (Regulation and Control) Rules.
- **Day 7+ (Joint Escalation)**: For persistent industrial or commercial establishment noise, escalate in writing to the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office, and the MPCB Regional Officer.

## Citizen Tip for Swift Resolution
In real-time nighttime emergencies, dialing 112 produces the fastest response because police patrol vehicles are tracked via GPS. Inform the 112 operator if the venue is located near sensitive areas like hospitals or schools.
