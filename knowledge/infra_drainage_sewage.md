# Stormwater Drainage and Underground Sewerage Infrastructure

## Overview
Pune's wastewater and runoff management comprises two distinct infrastructure networks: the underground sanitary sewerage system designed for domestic and commercial wastewater collection, and the open or box-culvert stormwater drainage network (including roadside gutters and natural nullahs) engineered for rainwater discharge into the Mula and Mutha rivers.

## Underground Sewer Network
1. **Sewer Mains and Laterals**: Salt-glazed stoneware (SWG), reinforced cement concrete (RCC), or high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipes conveying wastewater by gravity.
2. **Manholes and Inspection Chambers**: Vertical access shafts placed at line intersections, diameter changes, and regular gradients.
3. **Sewage Treatment Plants (STPs)**: Centralized municipal facilities (such as Mundhwa, Naidu Hospital, Erandwane, Bhiroba, and Baner STPs) executing primary and secondary biological treatment.
4. **Engineering Standards**: VERIFY: self-cleansing velocity standards and minimum pipe slope requirements under CPHEEO guidelines.

## Stormwater Drain Network
- **Roadside RCC Box Drains**: Surface runoff conduits constructed along primary roads to prevent carriageway waterlogging.
- **Natural Nullahs and Culverts**: Open streams that act as primary natural arteries during monsoon downpours.
- **Cross-Connection Hazards**: Illegal discharges of sanitary sewage into open stormwater drains violate municipal and environmental regulations, leading to severe odor, siltation, and untreated river contamination.

## Suggested Triage Criteria
Suggested triage criteria, not an official standard:
- **P1 (Critical / Emergency)**: Missing manhole cover on an unlit road or pedestrian footway posing immediate drowning/fall risk; active sewage flooding inside living quarters.
- **P2 (High Priority)**: Overflowing manhole spilling across an active market road, school entrance, or major junction.
- **P3 (Medium Priority)**: Choked stormwater drain chamber causing localized knee-deep water accumulation during rain.
- **P4 (Low Priority)**: Routine request for chamber desilting outside the active monsoon period.
