# Water and Air Pollution Control Acts: Citizen Reporting Mechanisms

## Statutory Mandate
The prevention and control of industrial and urban pollution in Maharashtra is governed by:

2. **The Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981**: Formulated to improve air quality and control emission of air pollutants into the atmosphere. VERIFY: fact not present on fetched source page.

Both Acts empower the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) and State Pollution Control Boards—such as the Maharashtra Pollution Control Board (MPCB)—to monitor, sample, inspect, and enforce environmental standards.

## Consent Requirements for Discharges
- **Consent to Establish (CTE)** and **Consent to Operate (CTO)**: Industries, manufacturing operations, processing units, or commercial setups discharging sewage, trade effluent, or atmospheric emissions are legally obligated to obtain statutory consent from MPCB before operating.
- Discharging untreated chemical effluents directly into nullahs, storm drains, or rivers (such as Mula-Mutha river system) constitutes a violation subject to regulatory shutdown and criminal prosecution under environmental laws.
