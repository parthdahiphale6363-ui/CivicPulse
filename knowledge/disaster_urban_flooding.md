# Disaster Preparedness and Response: Urban Flooding

## Overview
Urban flooding in Pune is driven by intense short-duration rainfall (cloudburst-type events), rapid urbanization with high surface impermeability, and swelling river discharges from upstream reservoirs (Khadakwasla dam). National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) guidelines emphasize integrated urban flood management combining early meteorological forecasting, structural drainage enhancements, and community preparedness.

## Vulnerable Zones in Pune
- **Riverside Low-Lying Settlements**: Sinhagad Road (Ekta Nagari, Pulachi Wadi, Vitthal Nagar), Sangamwadi, Yerwada, and riverside colonies along the Mutha and Mula river channels.
- **Undersized Culverts and Chokepoints**: Natural nullahs traversing densely built peth areas, Dhanori, Hadapsar, and Vimannagar where channel cross-sections are constricted.
- **Subway and Underpass Depressions**: Vehicular and pedestrian subways prone to rapid water entrapment during downpours.

## NDMA Urban Flood Safety Guidelines
Based on NDMA IEC guidelines:
1. **Before the Flood**:
   - Keep emergency disaster kits ready with torch, battery radio, dry rations, drinking water, first aid supplies, and important documents sealed in waterproof bags.
   - Clear drains around homes and identify nearest higher-ground relief shelters.
2. **During the Flood**:
   - Do not walk or drive through moving flood waters; "turn around, don't drown".
   - Disconnect main electrical power switches and gas valves before evacuating.
   - Avoid contact with downed electric wires and submerged transformer substations.
3. **After the Flood**:
   - Do not enter damaged structures until certified safe by municipal engineers.
   - Drink only boiled or chlorinated water; discard food contaminated by floodwaters.

## Which Systems Fail First
General planning knowledge (civic systems failure hierarchy):
1. **Immediate Failure (0-2 hours)**: Stormwater roadside box drains choke at grate inlets; road underpasses and low-lying arterial bridges submerge, severing vehicular transport corridors.
2. **Secondary Failure (2-6 hours)**: Underground sewage networks back up into ground-floor tenements; localized electricity distribution substations are shut down by power utilities (MSEDCL) as a life-safety precaution.
3. **Tertiary Failure (6-24 hours)**: Municipal drinking water distribution pipelines suffer negative-pressure contamination; telecom cell towers exhaust battery backups if power grid remains submerged.

## Suggested Triage Criteria
Suggested triage criteria, not an official standard:
- **P1 (Critical / Emergency)**: People stranded or trapped on rooftops/vehicles by rapidly rising waters; water entering electrical substations or ICU hospital basements.
- **P2 (High Priority)**: Road underpass or vital arterial bridge submerged, blocking ambulance and emergency vehicle movement.
- **P3 (Medium Priority)**: Water accumulation in housing society parking lots or residential compounds without immediate life threat.
- **P4 (Low Priority)**: Post-flood road silt removal and debris clearance requests.
