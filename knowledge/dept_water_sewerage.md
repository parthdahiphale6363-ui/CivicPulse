# Water Supply and Drainage Department

## Scope and Mandate
The water supply and drainage functions of Pune Municipal Corporation manage potable water distribution and public drainage networks across Pune. The mandate covers distribution pipelines, valve chambers, underground sewerage collection, and maintenance.

## Sub-Issues and Complaint Taxonomy
- Burst water distribution mains, cracked feeder pipes, and valve chamber leaks.
- Contaminated drinking water supply (turbidity, discoloration, sewage odor).
- Complete disruption of scheduled water timings or low hydraulic pressure.
- Blocked underground sewer lines causing manhole surcharges onto streets.
- Damaged, sunken, or missing sewer manhole chambers.

- Video or photograph showing water bubbling or sewer overflowing.
- Time of typical supply hours in the colony.
- Consumer account number if related to dedicated water meter or bill.

## Common Delay Causes and Escalation
Excavation clearances needed from traffic police on congested corridors; underground pipelines entangled with other utility conduits.
- Level 1 Escalation: the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office.
- Level 2 Escalation: Assistant Municipal Commissioner of the ward office.
- Level 3 Escalation: Municipal Commissioner / Central Grievance Redressal Officer.

## Standard 3-Step Resolution Procedure
1. **Emergency Isolation and Safety Containment**: Valve operators isolate feeder gates to prevent water loss, placing barricades around leaks: VERIFY
2. **Excavation, Joint Repair, or Suction Jetting**: Jetting trucks clear blockages; repair teams excavate and fix fractured pipe sections: VERIFY
3. **Flushing, Testing, and Restoration Audit**: Repaired lines are flushed, pressure tested, trench backfilled, and ticket closed on PMC Care.
