# Municipal Water Supply Infrastructure and Distribution Network

## Overview
Pune's municipal water supply system draws raw water primarily from the upstream dam reservoirs on the Mutha river basin (Khadakwasla, Panshet, Varasgaon, and Temghar dams). Raw water is conveyed to major Water Treatment Plants (WTPs) including Parvati, Warje, Cantonment, Vadgaon, and Holkar Bridge, where it undergoes coagulation, sedimentation, rapid sand filtration, and chlorination before distribution to Elevated Storage Reservoirs (ESRs) and Ground Level Reservoirs (GLRs) across administrative wards.

## Network Components and Distribution
1. **Bulk Transmission Mains**: High-pressure trunk mains conveying treated water from WTPs to zonal balancing reservoirs.
2. **Elevated Storage Reservoirs (ESRs)**: Elevated tanks that maintain hydraulic pressure for gravity-fed distribution into residential distribution zones.
3. **Distribution Piping**: Secondary and tertiary feeder pipelines terminating in consumer service connections.
4. **Engineering Standards**: VERIFY: design per capita water supply norm in LPCD (litres per capita per day) and residual terminal pressure requirements.

## Common Infrastructure Failures
- **Trunk Main Bursts**: High-volume pressurized water releases resulting in localized flooding, road subsidence, and broad-scale supply cuts.
- **Joint and Valve Leaks**: Chronic low-volume leakages around air valves, sluice valves, and pipe joints causing non-revenue water loss and reduced tail-end pressure.
- **Contamination via Siphonage**: Low-pressure or dry pipe intervals allowing surrounding subsoil water or sewage ingress through pinhole leaks.

## Suggested Triage Criteria
Suggested triage criteria, not an official standard:
- **P1 (Critical / Emergency)**: Major transmission pipeline burst washing away road sub-base or cutting off water supply to an entire ward or hospital zone.
- **P2 (High Priority)**: Noticeable pipeline contamination resulting in foul-smelling or muddy tap water across multiple tenements.
- **P3 (Medium Priority)**: Distribution line leakage resulting in severely reduced pressure at tail-end lanes.
- **P4 (Low Priority)**: General inquiries regarding new water meter installation or individual billing discrepancies.
