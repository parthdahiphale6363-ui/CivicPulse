# Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act 2015 (RTS)

## Statutory Basis and Purpose
The Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act, 2015 (Maharashtra Act No. XXXI of 2015) was enacted to ensure transparent, efficient, and time-bound delivery of notified public services to citizens across the state of Maharashtra, including urban local bodies like Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC).

## Notified Services and Time-Bound Delivery
Under the Act, every public authority must notify the specific services provided to citizens, along with designated officers responsible for service delivery and the stipulated time limit for completion.
- Birth and Death Certificates: Subject to notified statutory time limits: VERIFY
- Property Tax and Water Tax No-Dues Certificates: Subject to notified statutory time limits: VERIFY
- Other municipal permissions and operational services: Rendered according to published notification schedules: VERIFY

## Structured Three-Tier Appeal Mechanism
If an eligible citizen is denied a notified service or experiences delays beyond the stipulated time limit without justified cause, the Act provides a three-tier appeal hierarchy:
1. **First Appeal**: Preferred before the First Appellate Authority (designated senior departmental officer at the municipal corporation or ward office).
2. **Second Appeal**: Preferred before the Second Appellate Authority if dissatisfied with the first appeal order.
3. **Third Appeal**: Preferred before the Maharashtra State Commission for Right to Service.

## Penalties for Administrative Default
If a Designated Officer fails to deliver a notified service within the stipulated time limit without reasonable cause, financial penalties may be imposed: VERIFY (Penalty range: ₹500 to ₹5,000 under statutory rules: VERIFY)
