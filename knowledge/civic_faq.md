# CivicPulse Platform FAQ (15 Key Questions)

### Q1: What is CivicPulse?
CivicPulse is an AI-powered municipal intelligence and problem-solving platform connecting citizens and urban administrations to report, track, and resolve civic infrastructure issues.

### Q2: How do I report a problem?
Navigate to `/report`, select the issue category, describe the problem, pinpoint the location on the interactive Leaflet map or via GPS, and submit multimedia evidence.

### Q3: How does multilingual voice reporting work?
Click the microphone button on the report form. Speak in Hindi, Marathi, English, or regional languages. Groq Whisper transcribes and auto-formats the complaint into clean municipal text.

### Q4: What does the Description 'AI Enhance' button do?
It analyzes your raw description draft, auto-detects the matching category, rates severity (1-10), and formats standard municipal wording.

### Q5: How does Location 'AI Enhance' work?
It takes raw location text and exact GPS coordinates, refines the address into standard postal form, and identifies nearby landmarks.

### Q6: What multimedia can I upload?
You can upload photo evidence or short videos (strictly 15 seconds or less, under 20MB) to provide visual proof for AI analysis.

### Q7: How does duplicate detection work?
When you select a location, CivicPulse scans nearby complaints within geographical distance and prompts you to upvote an existing report instead of creating duplicates.

### Q8: How does upvoting work?
Citizens can upvote existing complaints in their locality. Each user can upvote once per issue to highlight community urgency.

### Q9: Does upvoting affect complaint priority?
Yes. Highly upvoted complaints surface at the top of the community leaderboard and administrative dashboard for priority resolution.

### Q10: How do I track my complaint status?
Check `/profile` or `/complaints`. Each complaint shows its lifecycle status (Pending or Resolved) along with official admin responses.

### Q11: How does user registration and OTP verification work?
Users sign up with mobile and `@gmail.com`. A 6-digit verification code is delivered via email service to verify account authenticity.

### Q12: Can I report an issue anonymously?
Yes. Toggle "Report Anonymously" on the report form. Your username and identity will be hidden from the public feed.

### Q13: What does the SOS / Immediate Danger toggle do?
Reserve this for life-threatening hazards (live electric wires, sinkholes, gas leaks). The AI marks the issue Urgent for immediate dispatch.

### Q14: What is a Fix Streak?
Citizens earn bonus trust points by actively reporting and verifying issues over consecutive active days.

### Q15: How do administrators update complaints?
Authorized administrators draft formal responses, review AI resource blueprints, and mark complaints resolved with resolution timestamps.
