# Road and Public Works Department (Path Vibhag)

## Scope and Mandate
The road and public works functions of Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) oversee the resurfacing, structural maintenance, and safety compliance of public street networks across municipal wards. Responsibilities include arterial corridors, internal feeder lanes, footpaths, culverts, and utility trenching regulation.

## Sub-Issues and Complaint Taxonomy
- Potholes, surface craters, and asphalt raveling on bituminous roads.
- Concrete slab settlement, joint failures, and bridge expansion joints.
- Trenching reinstatement after underground utility works.
- Broken pedestrian curbstones, missing paver blocks, and open storm gutter gratings.
- Missing or illegible road directional signage and faded zebra crossings.

## Citizen Evidence Requirements
Citizens lodging complaints through PMC Care should provide:
1. Nearest identifiable landmark or chowk.
2. Clear photographs showing both the defect close-up and surrounding street perspective.
3. Approximate dimensions of damage.

## Common Delay Causes and Escalation
Monsoon rain restricts asphalt plant operations, requiring cold asphalt solutions. Uncoordinated utility digging frequently stalls repair schedules.
- Level 1 Escalation: the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office.
- Level 2 Escalation: Assistant Municipal Commissioner of the ward office.
- Level 3 Escalation: Municipal Commissioner / Central Grievance Redressal Officer.

## Standard 3-Step Resolution Procedure
1. **Triage and Safety Securing**: Field supervisor conducts on-site inspection after complaint intake on PMC Care and sets safety markers if hazard exists: VERIFY
