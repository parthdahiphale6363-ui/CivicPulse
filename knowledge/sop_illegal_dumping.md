# Standard Operating Procedure: Illegal Dumping and Debris Enforcement

## Category Overview and Scope
This standard operating procedure governs the detection, removal, and punitive enforcement against unauthorized dumping of municipal solid waste, commercial garbage, and Construction & Demolition (C&D) debris on riverbeds, vacant plots, and public rights-of-way under PMC jurisdiction.

## Service Level Agreement (SLA)
- Chronic roadside or riverbed garbage dumping: SLA timeline: VERIFY
- C&D debris dumping obstructing carriage-way: SLA timeline: VERIFY
- Open burning of dumped waste or plastic materials: SLA timeline: VERIFY

## Citizen Evidence Requirements
- Vehicle registration number (photograph of tipper/dumper truck) if observed in the act of illegal tipping.
- Clear photo showing the scale of dumped debris and surrounding survey number or street landmark.
- Timestamp and frequency of occurrences.

## Common Delay Factors
- Private ownership disputes where vacant land belongs to private developers or defense cantonment authorities.
- Requirement for heavy hydraulic excavators (JCB) and multi-axle dumpers to lift large concrete/rubble piles.

## 3-Step Standard Operating Procedure
1. **Field Inspection & Ownership Verification**: Ward Sanitary Inspector conducts a site visit, establishes whether the plot is public roadway or private property, and photographs the debris volume: VERIFY
2. **Mechanical Lifting & Transfer to C&D Plant**: A dedicated dumper truck and backhoe loader are mobilized to shovel debris and transport it to the municipal C&D processing facility: VERIFY
3. **Penal Challan & Fencing Notice**: Under Solid Waste regulations, a spot fine is levied against identified violators or notices issued to the landowner to fence the property: VERIFY

## Escalation Matrix
