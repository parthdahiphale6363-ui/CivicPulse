# Citizen Playbook: Unfixed Pothole Action Plan

## Scenario Overview
A dangerous road pothole or surface crater on a commuter road or residential feeder street within Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) limits that poses an immediate risk of vehicular accidents or tire damage.

## Confirmed Municipal Channels
- PMC Care Grievance Portal: https://complaint.pmc.gov.in/home
- PMC Central Toll-Free Helpline: 1800 1030 222 (Operating hours: 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM)
- Official WhatsApp / SMS Complaint Desk: +91-9689900002
- Grievance Email: feedback@punecorporation.org
- Field inspection: the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office

## Suggested Follow-up Cadence (Citizen Practice, Not an Official SLA)
The day-by-day milestones below represent practical steps followed by active citizen groups to ensure accountability; they do not constitute official statutory guarantees:
- **Day 1 (Evidence & Filing)**: Capture two clear photographs (one close-up showing crater depth, one wide-angle displaying nearby shops, street names, or chowk). File ticket on PMC Care under the relevant road maintenance category. Retain the generated numeric token reference.
- **Day 3 (First Status Check)**: Check complaint tracking via the PMC Care portal. If marked unassigned or uninspected, send an SMS/WhatsApp reminder with token number to +91-9689900002.
- **Day 5 (Ward Office Engagement)**: If no physical barricade or repair crew has visited, call toll-free helpline 1800 1030 222. Provide token number and request escalation through the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office.
- **Day 7+ (Formal Escalation)**: Submit a written complaint referencing the token number to the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office. If on an arterial transit corridor, request immediate safety measures for traffic safety.

## Citizen Tip for Swift Resolution
Include recognizable landmarks in the complaint title (e.g., "Pothole 50m before landmark chowk"). Specific spatial markers accelerate field crew dispatch significantly compared to generic colony names.
