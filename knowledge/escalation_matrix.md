# Municipal Grievance Escalation Matrix

## Typical Administrative Hierarchy (Confirm Names with Your Ward Office)
When civic complaints regarding roads, sanitation, water supply, streetlights, or drainage are not resolved within standard operating timelines, citizens can follow this typical hierarchy:

### Level 1: Field Operations (Junior Engineer / Sanitary Inspector)
- **Role**: Field execution squads, initial site inspection, contractor work orders, and day-to-day issue resolution.
- **Typical Designation**: Junior Engineer (Roads / Water / Electrical) / Ward Sanitary Inspector.
- **Channel**: Contact the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office.
- **Ward Desk Phone / Individual Officers**: Typical hierarchy; confirm names with your ward office: VERIFY.

### Level 2: Ward Administration (Ward Officer / Assistant Engineer)
- **Role**: Administrative and field oversight for the designated municipal ward (Kshetriya Karyalaya).
- **Typical Designation**: Ward Officer / Assistant Municipal Commissioner of the Ward.
- **Channel**: Local Ward Office (Kshetriya Karyalaya) or PMC CARE grievance token reference.
- **Ward Officer Direct Phone / Email**: Typical hierarchy; confirm names with your ward office: VERIFY.

### Level 3: Zonal Supervision (Deputy / Additional Municipal Commissioner)
- **Role**: Zonal supervision across multiple prabhags/wards, tender reallocation, and inter-departmental coordination.
- **Typical Designation**: Deputy Municipal Commissioner (Zonal Head) / Additional Municipal Commissioner.
- **Channel**: Written memorandum submitted to Zonal Municipal Office or PMC Central Headquarters.
- **Zonal Phone / Direct Email**: Typical hierarchy; confirm names with your ward office: VERIFY.

### Level 4: Municipal Leadership (Municipal Commissioner)
- **Role**: Chief Executive Officer of the Municipal Corporation responsible for overall administration.
- **Typical Designation**: Municipal Commissioner, Pune Municipal Corporation.
- **Channel**: Central grievance email: feedback@punecorporation.org or central toll-free helpline: 1800 1030 222.
- **Commissioner Direct Secretariat Line**: Typical hierarchy; confirm names with your ward office: VERIFY.

### Level 5: State & Central Grievance Redressal Mechanisms
- **Role**: External appellate and administrative grievance portals.
- **Platforms**: Aaple Sarkar Grievance Portal (Government of Maharashtra) and CPGRAMS (Government of India).
- **Statutory Appeals**: For notified public services, three-tier statutory appeals under Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act (RTS Act 2015).
- **State Portal / CPGRAMS Contact**: Typical hierarchy; confirm names with your ward office: VERIFY.
