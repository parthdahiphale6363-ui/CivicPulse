# Citizen Playbook: Dark Street and Faulty Streetlight Action Plan

## Scenario Overview
Streetlights along an entire road or outside a sensitive public location (school, college, bus stand, hospital) are non-functional, leaving the street in complete darkness and increasing night safety risks.

## Confirmed Municipal and Utility Channels
- PMC Care Grievance Portal: https://complaint.pmc.gov.in/home
- PMC Central Toll-Free Helpline: 1800 1030 222 (Operating hours: 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM)
- Official WhatsApp / SMS Complaint Desk: +91-9689900002
- MSEDCL (Mahavitaran) Power Outage Toll-Free Helpline: 1800-233-3435 or 1800-212-3435
- Field repair: the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office

## Suggested Follow-up Cadence (Citizen Practice, Not an Official SLA)
The milestones below represent community follow-up guidelines; they do not represent statutory municipal promises:
- **Night 1 (Logging & Identification)**: Walk down the unlit stretch and note any visible pole identification markers. Take a photograph showing the darkness. Register the complaint on PMC Care under the relevant electrical category. Record your token number.
- **Day 2 (Grid Outage vs Luminaire Fault)**: If nearby commercial shops and residential buildings also lost power, call MSEDCL on 1800-233-3435 to confirm whether an upstream distribution network issue exists. If only municipal streetlights are dark, message the PMC WhatsApp desk (+91-9689900002) with your ticket details.
- **Day 4 (Electrical Follow-up)**: If lights remain off after two nights, call PMC toll-free 1800 1030 222. Provide the token number and request status from the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office.
- **Day 7+ (Senior Officer Escalation)**: If multiple consecutive poles remain non-operational, follow up through the relevant PMC department, via PMC CARE or your ward office, or email feedback@punecorporation.org highlighting public safety.

## Citizen Tip for Swift Resolution
Pole numbers or nearest shop/building landmarks are the most critical data points for electrical crews. Providing landmarks allows technicians to locate the circuit quickly.
